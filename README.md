🐾 Bob & Reggie Rentals LLC
Premium Fleet Management & Private Rentals | Bend, Oregon

Welcome to the digital infrastructure for Bob & Reggie Rentals LLC. This repository houses the custom-built vehicle inspection and check-in application designed to support a future fleet of high-performance and technically durable rental vehicles in Central Oregon.

📈 Business Status: Pre-Launch
We are currently developing the operational framework, digital check-in tools, and insurance-compliant workflows required to manage a premium rental fleet. We are actively scouting vehicles that meet our standards for durability and innovation.

📱 Infrastructure Features
This application is designed to streamline the rental experience through:

Mobile-Optimized Inspections: A 30+ point photo capture system for comprehensive vehicle documentation.

Dual-Path Booking: Support for both marketplace (Turo) and direct private rentals.

Compliance Ready: Built-in logic for driver's license verification, digital signatures, and integrated payments.

🔑 Future Booking Options
Once the fleet is live, we will offer:

Turo Marketplace: Standard rentals with platform-backed protection and support.

Direct Business Rentals: Corporate and private rates for local rentals in Bend and the surrounding region.
